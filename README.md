# Kennedy Approach

A polished browser remake of the 1985 MicroProse air traffic control classic, styled after the Atari grid version.

**Game recreated by Mathew Doughty.**

**Play it:** https://delooze29-stack.github.io/kennedy-approach/

- 15 airports across the USA, Europe and the rest of the world, each with its own runway layout (1 to 5 stars).
- Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty (10, 6 and 4 strikes), plus Slow, Normal and Fast game speed.
- Light props, medium jets and heavies, each handling a little differently.
- Spoken pilot readbacks and a radio log you can switch on and off.

## How to play
- Orange planes are landing: bring them down to 1 and fly them along a runway.
- White and blue planes pass through: send them out of their DEST city at 5 (light aircraft at 3).
- Planes at the same height must never get close. Heavies need an extra square of room.
- Click a plane or press its letter, then use the arrow keys or scroll wheel (or click where you want it to go).
