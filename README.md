# Kennedy Approach

A polished browser remake of the 1985 MicroProse air traffic control classic, styled after the Atari grid version.

**Play it:** https://delooze29-stack.github.io/kennedy-approach/

- Orange planes are landing: bring them down to 1 and fly them along the ATL runway.
- White and blue planes pass through: send them out of their DEST city at 5.
- Planes at the same height must never get close. Three strikes and you're out.
- Click a plane or press its letter, then use the arrow keys (or click where you want it to go).
